FAQs

Answers to some of the questions visitors ask before starting a project.

What services does Frikret offer?

Frikret provides graphic design, web development, and UI/UX design. We help businesses create stronger visual identities, professional websites, and digital experiences that are easy to understand and use.

What is your design and development process?

We begin by understanding your business, goals, audience, and requirements. We then plan the solution, create the design or development work, review it with you, make the necessary improvements, and prepare the final deliverables.

How long does a project take?

Project timelines depend on the type and scope of the work. A simple design project may take a few days, while a complete website or larger digital project may require several weeks. We discuss the expected timeline before work begins.

How much does a project cost?

Every project is different, so pricing depends on the scope, complexity, number of deliverables, and requirements. Contact us with your project details and we can discuss the appropriate solution and provide a quotation.

Can you work with my existing brand?

Yes. We can work with your existing brand guidelines, colors, logo, and visual assets. We can also help improve or refresh your existing visual identity when needed.

How do I start a project with Frikret?

Simply contact us and tell us what you are trying to achieve, what you need, and any important requirements you already have. We will discuss your project with you and determine the best way to move forward.
